# AI Prompt — Transdental Single-Page Editorial Website

Create a **single-page website design concept** for **Transdental**, a company that distributes **dental units** and supporting dental clinic equipment, and also provides **installation, service, warranty support, maintenance, and consultation**.

The goal of this task is to explore the **design direction first**, not the full multi-page structure yet.

The website should feel like a **premium, editorial, modern dental equipment landing page** — not a generic corporate template, not a typical SaaS page, and not a beauty/skincare website.

This is **not an e-commerce website**. Do **not** display prices, carts, checkout, or payment flow. The primary conversion goal is to encourage visitors to contact Transdental through **WhatsApp**.

---

## Core Design Direction

Design a **single-page landing page** with these qualities:

- Premium
- Editorial
- Minimal
- Clean
- Clinical
- Modern
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Conversion-focused
- B2B product-distributor feel

The page should feel like a **brand-led landing page** for a modern dental equipment company.

It should not look like a standard distributor website.

---

## Important Creative Direction

Use a layout language inspired by **editorial landing pages** and **poster-like compositions**, especially in the hero section.

The hero section must **not** use a generic split layout such as “text on the left, image on the right.”

Instead, create a hero that feels like a **designed canvas**:

- oversized typography as a key visual element
- layered imagery
- floating micro-information
- generous white space
- subtle visual rhythm
- product-focused but artistic composition

Think of the hero like a **digital poster** rather than a standard business header.

---

## Brand Colors

Use colors inspired by the Transdental brand/logo.

Primary brand palette:

- Primary Navy: `#032B63`
- Cyan Accent: `#0294C9`
- Soft Accent Blue: `#7AC6E1`
- Off White: `#F6F7F8`
- Soft Gray Card: `#F1F3F5`
- Primary Text: `#181A1F`
- Muted Text: `#6B7280`
- Border Light: `#E5E7EB`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

### Color Usage Rules

- Use **off-white / light gray** as the dominant background.
- Use **dark text** for clarity and premium feel.
- Use **navy** for brand trust and structural accents.
- Use **cyan only as a small accent**, not as a dominant color.
- Avoid making the whole page heavily blue.
- Avoid obvious blue-to-cyan gradients.
- Avoid “AI-looking” glossy gradient-heavy sections.

The color approach should be:

- 80% neutral tones
- 15% dark/navy text or structure
- 5% cyan accent highlights

Use cyan only for:

- one keyword in a headline
- small active states
- small icons
- micro highlights
- links or CTA emphasis
- tiny details like a plus sign, underline, or bullet accents

---

## Typography Direction

Use a **modern editorial grotesk / geometric sans-serif** style.

The typography must feel:

- elegant
- clean
- premium
- slightly bold
- high-fashion editorial in layout, but still readable and professional

Avoid typography that feels:

- too startup-generic
- too playful
- too rounded and cute
- too futuristic
- too decorative

Typography style should include:

- very large hero headlines
- clear hierarchy
- short paragraphs
- large spacing
- minimal clutter
- bold but refined word placement

The typography is one of the main design tools.

## Hero Section Requirements

This is the most important part.

Design a **non-generic editorial hero**.

### Hero Layout Principles

Do **not** use:

- standard left-right hero
- generic stock-photo-with-text layout
- giant gradient background
- typical SaaS hero structure

Do use:

- oversized headline spanning large area
- product visual layered behind or around text
- floating micro information elements
- rounded outer container or framed canvas if helpful
- subtle spatial composition
- a soft clinical product image as part of the visual system

### Hero Visual Direction

Use a **large, soft, bright dental-related object** as the hero visual.

For example:

- dental unit silhouette
- dental chair close-up
- dental handpiece close-up
- dental equipment detail
- clean clinic equipment imagery

The visual should be:

- soft
- bright
- low-contrast
- slightly desaturated
- lightly blended into the background
- premium and clinical

It should **not** feel like a random stock photo.

A strong option is:

> a large semi-transparent dental unit or dental chair visual placed behind oversized typography, softly blended into the off-white background.

### Hero Content Direction

Suggested structure:

- small eyebrow label
- huge editorial headline
- short supporting paragraph
- WhatsApp CTA
- secondary CTA or subtle link
- floating micro labels such as:
  - Dental Unit Distributor
  - Installation Support
  - Service & Warranty
  - Clinic Equipment
  - After-Sales Care

### Suggested Headline Direction

Use a bold, oversized headline related to Transdental’s positioning.

Examples of direction:

- **Dental Units for Modern Clinics**
- **Smarter Dental Equipment for Growing Clinics**
- **Dental Equipment, Installed with Confidence**
- **Clinical Equipment for Modern Dental Practice**

You may highlight one word in cyan, but do not overdo it.

### Hero CTA

Primary CTA:

**Consult via WhatsApp**

Secondary CTA:

**Explore Services** or **See Product Focus**

## Single-Page Structure

Build the page as a **single-page design concept** with these sections.

### 1. Hero Section
A bold editorial hero with oversized headline, layered dental unit imagery, and subtle micro information.

### 2. Editorial About / Mission Section
A section that introduces Transdental with a large mission statement.

Suggested direction:

- left side: large text block or statement
- right side: soft product/clinic image
- below: 2–3 large soft cards with short themes

Suggested content themes:

- Product Guidance
- Installation Support
- Comfortable Clinic Setup
- Reliable After-Sales Care

This section should feel spacious and elegant.

### 3. Product & Service Bento Section
Instead of a standard equal grid, create a **bento-style editorial card layout**.

Use different card sizes.

Suggested items:

- Dental Unit (featured large card)
- Installation & Setup
- Service & Maintenance
- Warranty Support
- Procurement Consultation
- Spareparts & Supporting Equipment

The section should feel visual and premium, not text-heavy.

### 4. Process Section
Create a minimal and clean explanation of how Transdental works.

Suggested steps:

1. Consultation
2. Product Recommendation
3. Quotation
4. Delivery & Installation
5. After-Sales Support

This can be a horizontal editorial timeline or a clean numbered section.

### 5. Why Transdental Section
Use a simple but strong layout.

Instead of many small feature cards, use a refined structure such as:

- large section heading
- 3 short value blocks with numbering

For example:

- 01 Product-focused Guidance
- 02 Installation & Technical Support
- 03 Reliable After-Sales Response

Make it feel curated, not crowded.

### 6. Final CTA Section
Create a strong closing CTA.

But keep it visually elegant.

Avoid a loud SaaS-style CTA block.

Suggested CTA message:

**Looking for the right dental unit for your clinic?**

Supporting text:

**Talk to Transdental for product consultation, installation support, and after-sales assistance.**

CTA button:

**Chat on WhatsApp**

### 7. Footer
Create a clean, premium footer.

Include:

- Transdental name/logo
- short description
- WhatsApp
- contact placeholder
- quick section links

Optional visual idea:
At the very bottom, use a very large typographic treatment of:

**TRANSDENTAL**

This can act as a signature visual ending, similar to a poster-style footer.

- clean spacing

## Animation Direction

Use only subtle motion.

Allowed motion:

- fade-up on scroll
- soft hover effect on cards
- subtle button hover
- smooth reveal of large headings
- gentle parallax-like image motion if minimal

Avoid:

- excessive animation
- loud transitions
- heavy motion effects
- gimmicky interactions
